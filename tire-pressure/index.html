ETRTO Sizing

ETRTO is a tire sizing standard from the European Tyre and Rim Technical Organisation. It gives a tire's size as two numbers in millimeters, width first and bead seat diameter second. So 70-584 means a tire about 70 mm wide that fits a rim with a 584 mm bead seat diameter.

The first number is the tire width—the tire's nominal width on a typical rim, so 70 mm is roughly a 2.8". It's the inch width converted to millimeters, give or take rounding. A tire's real width changes with the rim, and a wider inner rim spreads it out a little.

The second number tells you exactly which rim diameter a tire fits, which inch sizes don't always do. The three common MTB sizes are 559 for 26", 584 for 27.5" and 622 for 29". Any tire and rim with the same bead seat number fit each other, whatever the inch label says.

Notes

  • Wider tires and wider rims both call for less pressure, and rim width matters more than most riders think. Each 5 mm of inner rim width is worth about 1 to 1.3 psi at trail-bike pressures.
  • More weight calls for more pressure, but doubling the weight does not double the pressure.
  • Tougher casings hold their shape, so they can run 5–10% less pressure.
  • Tubes need a little more pressure than tubeless to avoid pinch flats, and hookless rims a little less.
  • Wet trails call for 10% less pressure.

Based on SRAM's AXS tire pressure guide (for Zipp wheels); not SRAM's official tool. A starting point only: never exceed the tire's or rim's maximum pressure.

SRAM's guide labels these rim types Tubes (Crochet), Hooks (Tubeless Crochet), and Hookless (Tubeless Straight Side).
